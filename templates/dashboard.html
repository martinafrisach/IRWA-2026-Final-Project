Physical sessions and missions

A physical session (sit-down) is a period of continuous use: a new one starts after 30 minutes without activity. Inside it, a mission groups consecutive queries with the same goal: a query that shares at least one (stemmed) term with the current mission continues it (e.g. "blue jeans" → "blue jeans slim"); otherwise a new mission starts.
